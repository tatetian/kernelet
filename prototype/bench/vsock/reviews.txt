Outcome: blocking findings; no reviewer concluded fundamental infeasibility.

Changes made in response:
- L1: defer EOF/read readiness while a pre-shutdown TX reservation remains;
  add controlled admitted-write/shutdown and survivor-span/death checks.
- S1/L2/E1: enforce an independent 64-connection limit and distinguish the
  8 MiB payload bound from allocator-rounded physical storage.
- E2: specify a non-coalescing reference copy path and identify optional
  Linux tiny-payload coalescing copies.
- E3: trace both Linux virtio/vhost notification directions, implemented
  carrier waits, and planned remote-CPU readiness/IPI/watch/upcall delivery.
- E4: add direct-vOSTD API-peer measurements at 4/16/64 KiB, separated from
  tenant end-to-end numbers and with clear limitations on attribution.
- E5: add a separate control connection to deterministically enable
  consumption and verify that it wakes a full-ring writer.
- E6/E7: document interrupted setup/file-reference semantics and fix typo.
