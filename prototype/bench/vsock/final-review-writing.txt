Required editorial fixes
1. Break up paragraphs whose opening sentence does not summarize their content.
   - Socket lifecycle, line 134: the first sentence describes the bounded listen
     queue, but the ten-sentence paragraph also specifies connect completion,
     errors, listener closure, signals, carrier termination, and descriptor-close
     cancellation. Split at least into setup/backlog behavior and interruption/
     cancellation paragraphs. Give each a sentence summarizing that unit, e.g.
     "Connection setup uses a bounded listener queue and defined failure modes."
     and "Blocked setup calls follow the caller's existing interruption rules."
   - Safe vOSTD interfaces, line 152: the first sentence introduces memory readers
     and writers, while later sentences specify sleeping-context restrictions and
     binary compatibility. Split copying, sleeping contract, and test-application
     compatibility into distinct short paragraphs; keep the host adapter nearby.
   - Shutdown, death, and storage lifetime, line 207: a write-shutdown opening
     leads into read shutdown, close, and forced death. Use a summary sentence
     covering graceful shutdown and terminal outcomes, or separate graceful
     shutdown/close from reset-on-death behavior.
   - Results and CPU accounting, line 378: split endpoint CPU from outer-task CPU;
     the endpoint-scope opening does not summarize QEMU/vhost accounting.
   - Results and CPU accounting, line 382: split sampling-window boundaries from
     halt polling. The timer-window opening does not summarize the configured
     idle CPU cost that forms the second half of the paragraph.
   These are local prose fixes, not reasons to restructure the proposal.

2. Introduce central terms/background before technical first use.
   - The copy diagram names "the broker" at line 57, before the broker is
     introduced explicitly. Define it in Summary or "A connection and its
     storage": the trusted endovisor component that owns connection storage,
     checks endpoint authority, and manages reservations. This also makes the
     trust boundary easier to grasp before details of handles and locking.
   - Move the existing "Linux: socket buffers" background (lines 37-41) before
     line 35, which already requires socket-buffer storage and scatterlists.
   - At line 342, briefly explain MSI-X and why separate interrupt vectors allow
     the data queue's target CPU to be checked. A focused background box at its
     first use follows AGENTS.md and lets an OS-course reader understand why the
     modern-device/legacy-interrupt distinction matters to the comparison.
   - "Lease" first appears at line 183 as an unexplained synonym for reservation.
     Introduce "reservation (lease)" once in the reserve/copy/publish section, or
     consistently use "reservation" throughout the explanatory text.

Confirmed strengths and limits
- Overall organization proceeds from motivation/reference mechanisms to the
  proposed storage and socket lifecycle, safety/lifetime, alternatives,
  implementation, methods, results, and remaining work.
- bind/listen/connect/accept, bounded backlog, runtime authority, CID identity,
  shutdown, close, cancellation, and death all have a stated place in the model.
- The copy account distinguishes scatterlist descriptors from payload storage,
  specifies the Linux reference path, and explicitly preserves application
  copies, optional coalescing, and the source-versus-dynamic-audit limitation.
- The historical Chapter 12.1 frame move is a distinct ownership alternative;
  it is not conflated with sharing broker-owned stream storage or given current
  performance credit. Provenance is retained.
- Carrier parking is clearly implemented; task-level readiness and event
  dispatch are clearly planned. The page does not claim to measure a faster
  virtual-interrupt delivery path.
- Reserve suppression and the combined poll-separation/demand-gating change are
  separated in the ablation. Pre-implementation independent review is stated and
  linked to retained reports; the mode-2 package is not over-decomposed.
- The favorable headline retains the 4 KiB virtio win. Pipe goodput is not called
  a bound, real virtio placement is disclosed, and native deployment differences
  and uncontrolled load/frequency are adjacent to the methods/results.
- Endpoint and outer CPU scopes are explicitly overlapping and are never added;
  asynchronous backend work, halt polling, and timing windows are disclosed.
- Historical initial results, the contemporary mode-0 baseline, instrumented
  diagnostics, and the final matched comparison are distinguishable.
- Final regression still being collected is not a review blocker when outcomes
  are linked to retained evidence upon completion, as the task explicitly allows.
